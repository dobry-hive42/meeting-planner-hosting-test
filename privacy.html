How we handle your data

Meeting Planner · last updated 13 November 2026

Who is responsible

Hive42 s.r.o., IČO 07859490, Bedlová 61, Pitkovice, 104 00 Praha 22, Czech Republic. Questions and requests: info@hive42.cz.

What we store and why

  • Your name, e-mail and your answers (which dates or times suit you), the language you use, and when you joined or changed them.
  • We use them only to find a date for the meeting you joined. The legal basis is your consent, which you give when you join.
  • Your e-mail is used only to send you your personal link, so you can change your answers from another device.

Who sees it

  • Everyone in the same poll sees your name and your answers.
  • Your e-mail is seen only by the organiser of the poll. It is never shown to the other people in the poll.

Where it is kept

  • The answers are stored in a Google Sheet in the organiser's Google Workspace account and processed by Google Apps Script. Google acts as a processor.
  • This page is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address for security reasons.
  • The page loads nothing from other services: no fonts, no trackers, no adverts.

How long

Names, e-mails and answers are erased automatically 6 months after the last date offered in the poll. On request we erase them earlier.

Your rights

You can ask what we store about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing doesn't affect what happened before. Write to info@hive42.cz. You can also complain to the data protection authority (in Czechia: Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz).

Cookies and browser storage

  • We use no cookies.
  • Your browser keeps one key that tells this page which answers are yours, and the language you chose, so you don't have to sign in. It is needed for the page to work, so it doesn't need your consent. You can delete it by clearing this site's data in your browser; you can then use the link from your e-mail again.
  • We don't measure visits and don't show adverts. If we ever add visit statistics, we will ask for your consent first.
